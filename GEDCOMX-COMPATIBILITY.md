# GEDCOM X compatibility

**Verdict: No, not directly.** Ahnen documents its model as a GEDCOM 7 projection (`README.md:133-178`). Some basic people and family data can be mapped, but the model is narrower and structurally different from GEDCOM X; it is not a GEDCOM X interchange model.

| Area | Main mismatch |
|---|---|
| Relationships and facts | `Family` groups partners, children, and family events; GEDCOM X uses typed, directional pairwise `Relationship`s. Person/family events are embedded, while GEDCOM X distinguishes independent `Event`s (with participant roles) from `Fact`s attached to people or relationships. Ahnen has no `Fact` or event-role model. (`Ahnen.Core/Models/FamilyTree.cs:46-64, 118-140`) |
| Names and gender | Names are limited to given/surname and a few GEDCOM fields; GEDCOM X names have ordered, localized forms and qualified parts. `Sex` is a closed GEDCOM enum, not GEDCOM X's `Gender` conclusion with a URI type. (`Ahnen.Core/Models/FamilyTree.cs:5-18, 74-84`; `Ahnen.Core/Models/GedcomEnums.cs:5-13, 60-71`) |
| Sources and evidence | No source descriptions/citations, source references on conclusions, evidence links, identifiers, attribution, or analysis are modeled. Notes and media are plain strings/file references rather than GEDCOM X `Note` and `SourceReference` structures. |
| Places and dates | Places are unstructured strings. Dates use a restricted GEDCOM 7 string subset; GEDCOM X dates support richer signed-year, range, recurrence, and approximate-date forms. (`Ahnen.Core/Models/FamilyTree.cs:46-55`; `Ahnen.Core/Models/GedcomDate.cs:6-10, 16-31`) |
| Extensibility | Closed enums and fixed fields do not provide GEDCOM X's URI-based vocabularies and extension properties. Unknown concepts cannot be preserved as typed values. |

These differences are grounded in the [GEDCOM X conceptual model](https://github.com/FamilySearch/gedcomx/blob/master/specifications/conceptual-model-specification.md) and its referenced [date](https://github.com/FamilySearch/gedcomx/blob/master/specifications/date-format-specification.md), [event](https://github.com/FamilySearch/gedcomx/blob/master/specifications/event-types-specification.md), [fact](https://github.com/FamilySearch/gedcomx/blob/master/specifications/fact-types-specification.md), [relationship](https://github.com/FamilySearch/gedcomx/blob/master/specifications/relationship-types-specification.md), and [name-part qualifier](https://github.com/FamilySearch/gedcomx/blob/master/specifications/name-part-qualifiers-specification.md) specifications. Supporting GEDCOM X would require a distinct model or a richer intermediate representation; a simple field-for-field rename would lose semantics and data.
